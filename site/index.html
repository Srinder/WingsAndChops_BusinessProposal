Our journey

From a first store to a franchise-ready brand.

  1. 2015
    01 / The beginning

    A neighborhood favorite takes shape

    Wings & Chops began as Wings Express Inc., opening its first store at Circle-C Mall on Congressional Avenue in Quezon City. Signature wings, chicken shots, and breaded pork chops brought a fresh take on comfort food to the local market.

  2. 2016
    02 / The foundation

    Consistency built from the inside

    The company established its own commissary in Tandang Sora, Quezon City, to support consistent quality and efficient food preparation and supply across all branches.

  3. 2020
    03 / The next chapter

    Sharing the brand through franchising

    Growing demand and market potential led Wings & Chops to franchising, sharing its success with aspiring entrepreneurs while staying true to quality, flavor, and service excellence.
